About

The goal of Solver is to easily solve any homework problem. Using our intuitive interface, enter your homework problem to instantly get a step-by-step solution. If you don't have a problem to solve, you can still learn by choosing an example. Try changing values to see how the solution changes. At the bottom of each page, click on definition, formula and explanation to get more information on the topic. If you can't find what you're looking for, send us an e-mail by clicking on the link below. Either we will let you know where to find it or work on making it to available to you in the future.
