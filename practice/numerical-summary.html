Hint
Examples:

Mean


1. Suppose that ∑x = 20 and n = 5. What is the sample mean?


Solution: 20/5 = 4


2. Suppose that x̄ = 4 and n = 3. What is ∑x?


Solution: 3 * 4 = 12


3. Consider a sample with values 2, 3 and 1. What is the sample mean?

Solution: (2+3+1)/3=2


Median


4. Consider a sample with an odd number of values. The three middle values, when the data are sorted, are 12, 17 and 21. What is the median?


Solution: 17


4. Consider a sample with an even number of values. The two middle values, when the data are sorted, are 4 and 8. What is the median?


Solution: (4 + 8)/2 = 6


6. Consider a sample with values 2, 3 and 1. What is the median?


2


Mode


7. Consider a sample with values 8, 2, 5, 2 and 9. What is the mode?


Solution: 2


8. Consider a sample with values 7, 1, 4, 3 and 5. What is the mode?


Solution: No Mode


9. Consider a sample with values 3, 6, 1, 8, 3 and 6. What are the modes?


Solution: 3 and 6


Percentiles


10. Consider a sample with 10 values and you want to find the 20th percentile. What is the index?


Solution: (20/100)*10 = 2


11. Suppose the index is equal to 3.5. You should take the value in which position?


Solution: 4


12. Suppose the index is equal to 6. You should average the values in positions 6 and what?


Solution: 7



Range


13. Consider a sample with values 3, 2, 6, 1, 8. What is the range?


Solution: 8 - 1 = 7


14. Suppose the range is 6 and the largest value is 15. What is the smallest value?


Solution: 15 - 6 = 9


14. Suppose the range is 5 and the smallest value is 11. What is the largest value?


Solution: 11 + 5 = 16


Interquartile Range


15. Suppose that Q1 = 5 and Q3 = 9. What is the IQR?


Solution: 9 - 5 = 4


16. Suppose that IQR = 6 and Q3 = 14. What is the Q1?


Solution: 14 - 6 = 8


17. Suppose that IQR = 3 and Q1 = 7. What is the Q3?


Solution: 7 + 3 = 10


Variance


18. Consider a sample with values 3, 2, 6, 1, 8. What is the range?


Solution: 8 - 1 = 7


19. Suppose the range is 6 and the largest value is 15. What is the smallest value?


Solution: 15 - 6 = 9


20. Suppose the range is 5 and the smallest value is 11. What is the largest value?


Solution: 11 + 5 = 16


Standard Deviation


21. Suppose that Q1 = 5 and Q3 = 9. What is the IQR?


Solution: 9 - 5 = 4


22. Suppose that IQR = 6 and Q3 = 14. What is the Q1?


Solution: 14 - 6 = 8


23. Suppose that IQR = 3 and Q1 = 7. What is the Q3?


Solution: 7 + 3 = 10
