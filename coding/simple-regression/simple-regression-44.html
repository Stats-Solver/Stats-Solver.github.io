Formula:

$$ \hat{y} = b_0 + b_1x $$

Example:

$$ x: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 $$

$$ b_0 = 6 \quad b_1 = -1 $$

Functions:

c(): creates an empty list of numbers

Explanation:

The first step in a hypothesis test is to calculate the test statistic. The test statistic is writting z because it follows the z distribution, also known as the standard normal distribution.
