Coding Confidence Intervals

$ z_{\alpha/2} $

Formula:

None

Example:

$$ \bar{x} = 5 \quad \sigma = 2 \quad n = 30 $$

$$ \text{Confidence Level} = 95 \% $$

Functions:

qnorm(p, lower.tail)

Explanation:

$ z_{\alpha/2} $ is the $z$ value providing an area of $\alpha / 2$ in the upper tail of the standard normal distribution. To use the area in the upper tail, set the argument lower.tail equal to FALSE.
