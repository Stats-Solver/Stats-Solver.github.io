How it Works:

The concept of a determinant can be illustrated by examining the solution of a system of linear equations. In a system of two equation in two unknowns, the denominator of the solutions are identical. Their value is called the determinant. The determinant of a 2x2 matrix can be calculated by subtracting the products of the elements on opposite sides diagonally.
